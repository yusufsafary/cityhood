Cityhood NFTs are digital collectibles: pieces of artwork recorded on Robinhood Chain. They are not securities, shares, currency, or any form of investment product. Buying or minting one should be treated as collecting artwork, not as an investment.

Nothing in this project promises that a Cityhood NFT will be worth anything after mint, that a secondary market will exist, that any marketplace will keep listing the collection, or that floor prices will rise. Blockchain and platform risks

  • Transactions on Robinhood Chain are irreversible. A mint sent to a wrong address or from a wrong wallet cannot be undone by the project.
  • Minting happens on OpenSea via SeaDrop. OpenSea availability, fees, and interface behaviour are outside project control.
  • Smart contracts carry risk, including bugs that audits and testing may not catch.
  • Wallet security is your responsibility. This project will never ask for your seed phrase or private key, on this site or anywhere else. Anyone asking is impersonating the project.
  • Network congestion, RPC failures, and ETH price movement can change mint costs, including the ETH equivalent of the $0.50 WL and $1.50 Public prices.
