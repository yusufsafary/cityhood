Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and how long it stays. Short version: almost nothing, and only for the allowlist.

Data collected at registration

This site does not run registration forms and collects no entry data. Raffle entries are public comments on the project X account, made under X's own privacy policy. For the draw, the project processes exactly two items from those public comments:

  • Your EVM wallet address. Wallet addresses are public blockchain identifiers.
  • Your X username, used only to verify the follow and repost requirements.

No names, emails, passwords, private keys, seed phrases or payment details are collected. This site never asks you to connect a wallet or sign a message.

How registration data is used

  • Checking that registration requirements (follow and repost) are met.
  • Running the GTD and WL raffle and building the SeaDrop allowlists.
  • Publishing winner wallet addresses on this site so anyone can verify results with the status checker. Winner lists contain wallet addresses only, never X usernames.

Registration data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Status checker

Addresses typed into the status checker are compared against the published winner lists in your browser. Checker lookups are not stored by this site.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting. Standard server and content-delivery logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) may be generated by the hosting provider by default and are used only for security and operation of the site.

Third-party services

Links on this site lead to third parties including OpenSea, X, and the Blockscout explorer. Those services run under their own privacy policies. Minting on OpenSea is governed by OpenSea's terms and privacy policy, not by this one.

Retention

Registration records are kept until the raffle and mint stages conclude and results are final. After that, records that are not part of the published winner lists are deleted. Published winner wallet addresses remain visible as the public record of the draw.

Your choices

Registration is voluntary. If you do not want your wallet address on a winner list, do not enter the raffle; Public mint needs no entry and collects nothing here. Questions about this policy can be sent through the project X account, @appcityhood.
