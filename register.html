Registration

GTD and WL spots

Registration for Cityhood mint stages happens on this page and nowhere else. Status right now: registration opening soon. Dates will be announced on the project X account and posted here. Public mint needs no registration.

Requirements

Before you register

  • Follow the project X account. Handle announced soon. Registrations from non-followers do not qualify.
  • Repost the announcement post. Repost, not just a like. Both checks run against the X username you submit.
  • Use one wallet you control. Winners mint from the exact address registered here. Addresses cannot be swapped after the draw.
  • Public address only. Registration asks for a public wallet address and an X username. No wallet connection, no signature, no payment on this site, ever.
