Doc. 01 / 10 · Raffle

The raffle runs on X

GTD and WL allocation for the Cityhood mint is raffled on X, not on this site. There is no form here anymore: you enter by commenting your wallet address on the official raffle post. This page explains entry, the draw, and where winners are published.

Go to @appcityhood on X About the project

Entry

How to enter

  • Follow the project X account. Follow @appcityhood on X. Entries from non-followers do not qualify.
  • Repost the official raffle post. Repost, not just a like.
  • Comment your wallet address. One comment on the official post with one EVM wallet address (0x...). That comment is your entry for both stages.
  • Use one wallet you control. Winners mint from the exact address in their comment. Addresses cannot be swapped after the draw.

The official raffle post is published by @appcityhood only. Entry deadlines are stated in that post, in UTC. Any other post, account, or site collecting wallet addresses for Cityhood allocation is unofficial.

Prizes

What the raffle issues

Two permit types, drawn from one entry list. Winning GTD does not block a WL win.

GTD Free
Allocation 500 spots Cap max 1 / wallet Allocation guaranteed

A GTD win guarantees one free mint in the GTD stage on OpenSea.

WL $0.50
Allocation 1,000 spots Cap max 2 / wallet Paid in ETH at mint

A WL win unlocks the $0.50 stage on OpenSea, ahead of Public.

Unminted GTD and WL supply rolls into Public (2,833 supply, $1.50, max 3 per wallet, open to everyone, no entry needed).

The draw

How the draw works

When entries close, all wallet addresses from the comments are collected. Duplicates and invalid addresses are removed, and the final entrant list is published on this site before the draw, so anyone can check their entry is in.

Winners are drawn with a public seed: the hash of a specific future block, fixed by block number in advance. The draw is reproducible: given the published entrant list and the seed, anyone can recompute the same winners. 500 GTD winners are drawn first, then 1,000 WL winners from the same list.

Winner lists are published at cityhood.fun/data/status.json, announced on X, and checkable below.
