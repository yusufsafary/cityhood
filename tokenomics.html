$CITY, by numbers

$CITY is the utility token of Cityhood on Robinhood Chain. Fixed supply, minted once, published allocation. This page is the full accounting; the Utility page explains how the claim works.

Supply

Total supply is 10,000,000,000 $CITY, created in a single mint at deployment. The contract has no mint function after deployment: supply can only shrink, through the burn of unclaimed claim allocation.

Claim pool math

Full claim demand, if every block is minted and claimed, is 1,853,000,000 $CITY: 3,046 Blocks at 250,000 (761,500,000), 1,063 Districts at 500,000 (531,500,000) and 224 Landmarks at 2,500,000 (560,000,000). The 2,000,000,000 claim allocation covers that demand in full, with 147,000,000 of headroom.

Burn

When the claim window closes, $CITY still sitting in the claim allocation is burned: unclaimed block claims and the unused headroom. The burn is executed on-chain and announced on X with the transaction hash.

Status

The $CITY contract is in preparation and has not been deployed yet. Nothing on this page is a live market figure: there is no pool, no price and no trading until the token launches. Any $CITY offered for sale before the contract address is published on this site is not Cityhood's token.
