Process

Sec. 05 / 05

From block to $CITY

Minting lives on OpenSea. Claiming lives here. Three steps, and holding is the only requirement after mint.

Step 01

Own a block

Mint on the official OpenSea drop page via SeaDrop. Public opens today at 14:00 UTC: full 4,333 supply, max 25 per wallet, $1.50 in ETH equivalent.

Step 02

Hold it

Keep the block in your wallet. A claim belongs to a block, not to a person: if a block changes hands before its claim is taken, the claim travels with the block.

Step 03

Claim $CITY

When the claim opens, each block claims its tier rate once: 250,000 for a Block, 500,000 for a District, 2,500,000 for a Landmark.

Status

Public mint opens today.

Public stage is configured on-chain and opens today at 14:00 UTC, open to all, max 25 per wallet. GTD and WL permits are raffled on X: comment your wallet on the official post to enter, winners drawn publicly. Every minted block then earns its $CITY claim.
