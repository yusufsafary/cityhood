Process

How it works

Registration lives here. Minting lives on OpenSea. Three steps, no wallet connection on this site, no payment on this site.

Step 01

Register here

Once registration opens, submit your wallet address and X username on the Register page. Follow and repost the project X account to qualify.

Step 02

Raffle draw

Winners are drawn by raffle from valid registrations: 500 GTD spots and 1,000 WL spots. After the draw, check any wallet with the status checker.

Step 03

Mint on OpenSea

Winners mint on the official OpenSea drop page via SeaDrop when their stage opens. Public mint follows for everyone.
