Cityhood Games

Cityhood Games is the play layer of the city: an upcoming utility for block holders, built on the same grid as the collection. Your blocks are your pieces. Status: in preparation.

COMING SOON

Cityhood Games is in preparation. No title, date, or rules are announced yet, on purpose: games open after the mint, and first details are announced on X (@appcityhood) and on this page first.

Utility, stacked

Holding a block carries a stack of utility, in order. First, the $CITY claim: every block claims $CITY once, at its tier rate, after the mint. Second, Cityhood Games: blocks become pieces in games built for the city, and holders play first. More layers are announced here as they are locked, never before.

How games will work

Games are played with blocks you hold in your wallet: no extra purchase, no separate pass. A Block, a District, and a Landmark are different pieces, and tier matters in play just as it does in the claim. Anything beyond that, formats, seasons, and rewards, is announced when it is ready, on X and on this page.
