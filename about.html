About

A city, owned block by block

Cityhood is a collection of 3,333 NFTs on Robinhood Chain. Every piece is one city block, drawn as an isometric diorama: matte black buildings, lime-lit windows, pure black ground. Tagline says it plainly: every block has an owner.

Collection

Supply is fixed at 3,333 pieces under the symbol CITY. Artwork comes in three scales. Blocks are single low-rise blocks and form the common tier. Districts gather towers around a glowing plaza and form the rare tier. Landmarks are single monumental towers and form the legendary tier, the scarcest pieces in the collection.

One visual rule runs through all 3,333 pieces and through this website: ink black first, lime second, nothing else. Logo, hero art and NFTs all read from the same district grid.

Chain

Cityhood lives on Robinhood Chain, an Arbitrum Orbit layer 2 with chain ID 4663. Gas is paid in ETH. Contracts and transfers can be inspected on the Blockscout explorer. Drop contract address will be published on this site and on the project X account before mint, and only those announcements count as official.

Mint model: SeaDrop on OpenSea

Minting happens only on OpenSea, through SeaDrop, OpenSea's primary drop protocol. This website never sells NFTs, never takes payment and never asks you to connect a wallet or sign anything. Its jobs are information, registration and status checking.

GTD and WL access is earned before mint. You register a wallet address and X username here, follow and repost the project X account, and winners are drawn by raffle from valid registrations. Winner lists publish on the Register page, and the same addresses form the allowlist for the SeaDrop stages. Public mint runs on OpenSea without any registration.

Royalty

Cityhood carries a 5% creator royalty on secondary sales, set in the contract via ERC-2981 and honoured by marketplaces that support it, including OpenSea.
