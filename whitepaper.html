Cityhood, in one paper

The short paper behind the project: what Cityhood is, how its artwork and mint work, and what $CITY is for. Mechanics only, no promises.

1. A city in 4,333 pieces

Cityhood is a collection of 4,333 NFTs on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). Each piece is one city block, drawn as an isometric diorama: matte black buildings, lime-lit windows, pure black ground. The collection is the whole city; owning a piece means owning one block of it, with one wallet as its single owner.

2. Artwork system

Every block is generated from a seed fixed at mint. Scale decides the tier: a Block (about 70% of supply, 3,046 pieces) is one low-rise block; a District (about 25%, 1,063 pieces) is a tower cluster around a lime plaza; a Landmark (about 5%, 224 pieces) is one monumental tower over a full block grid. Tier is a property of the piece itself, visible in its metadata, and it later sets the piece's $CITY claim rate.

3. Mint model

Minting runs on OpenSea through SeaDrop, the standard drop protocol. The sale was designed in three stages: GTD (500 spots, free, raffle), WL (1,000 spots, $0.50, raffle) and Public (2,833 supply, $1.50, open). GTD and WL permits are raffled on X: anyone enters by commenting a wallet address on the official post, and winners are drawn publicly from the comment list with a block-hash seed. Unminted permit supply rolls into the Public stage, max 3 per wallet, paid in ETH equivalent. This site never sells NFTs and never takes payment; minting happens only on the official OpenSea drop page.

4. $CITY utility

$CITY is the city's utility token, fixed supply 10,000,000,000. Its first utility is simple: every block claims $CITY once, at its tier rate (Block 250,000; District 500,000; Landmark 2,500,000). A claim belongs to the block and travels with it if the block is sold unclaimed. The claim opens after the mint; unclaimed $CITY is burned when the claim window closes. Allocation and full accounting are published on the Tokenomics page.

5. Ownership and royalty

Blocks are standard ERC-721 tokens with an ERC-2981 royalty of 5%. Ownership is fully on-chain and verifiable on the Robinhood Chain explorer. The token contract is published on the Docs page.

6. Status and scope

Live today: this website, the NFT contract, and full artwork and metadata for all 4,333 blocks. In progress: OpenSea drop configuration and the $CITY contract. Not promised: any price, listing, or return. Cityhood is a collectible with a token utility, and this paper describes mechanics only.
