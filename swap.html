POOL SOON

There is no $CITY pool yet, because $CITY has not launched. A pool opens at token launch, seeded from the 1,000,000,000 $CITY liquidity allocation (10% of supply), on a decentralized exchange on Robinhood Chain. Venue and launch time are announced on X (@appcityhood) and on this page first.

Before launch

Until the $CITY contract address is published on this site, any token calling itself $CITY or CITY on any exchange is not Cityhood's token. Do not buy it. The only official sources are cityhood.fun and the project X account, @appcityhood.

At launch

When the pool opens, this page will carry the pool link, the token contract address, and the pair details. The claim for block holders opens on the Utility page in the same launch window: every block claims its $CITY once, at its tier rate.
