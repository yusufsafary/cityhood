What this site is

Cityhood.fun is the information and registration site for Cityhood, a collection of 4,333 NFTs (symbol CITY) on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). This site publishes project information, collects GTD and WL registrations, and hosts a winner status checker. This site does not sell NFTs, does not process payments, and does not custody any assets.

Minting happens on OpenSea

All minting takes place on OpenSea through the SeaDrop protocol, under OpenSea's own terms and interface. Contract address, stage times and mint links are official only when published on this site and on the project X account. Links from any other source should be treated as unofficial.

Registration and raffle

  • Registration requires a valid EVM wallet address, an X username, following the project X account, and reposting the announcement post.
  • One registration per wallet per stage. Duplicate entries count once. Entries using wallets or accounts you do not control may be removed.
  • Winners are drawn by raffle from valid registrations after registration closes. A GTD win guarantees one free mint during the GTD stage. A WL win grants access to the WL stage at $0.50, up to 2 per wallet. Neither win obliges you to mint.
  • Registered addresses cannot be changed after the raffle. Winner lists published on this site are final.
  • Stage allocations are 500 GTD spots, 1,000 WL spots and 2,833 Public supply. Unminted GTD and WL supply rolls into Public.
