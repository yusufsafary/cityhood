Every block earns $CITY

$CITY is the utility token of Cityhood, issued on Robinhood Chain. A Cityhood block is more than a picture of a block: each one carries a one-time claim on $CITY, paid at a rate set by its tier. Hold the block, hold the claim.

How claiming works

One claim per block, ever. When the claim opens, the wallet holding a block can claim that block's $CITY in a single transaction. A claim belongs to the block, not to a person: if a block is sold before its claim is taken, the claim travels with the block to its new holder. Claimed blocks are marked on-chain and cannot claim twice.

There is no staking, no lockup and no points program. Ownership is the whole mechanism, in line with a simple rule for the city: every block has an owner, and every owner gets paid in $CITY once.

More utility: Cityhood Games

Beyond the claim, blocks are pieces in Cityhood Games, the play layer of the city, now in preparation. Holders play first; details are announced on X and on the Games page.

Status

The $CITY contract is in preparation and has not been deployed yet. The claim opens after the Cityhood mint completes, and the opening is announced on X and on this page first. When the claim window closes, $CITY left unclaimed in the claim allocation is burned.

Questions

Do I need to register to claim? No. Registration was for mint stages. Claiming needs only a block in your wallet.

When can I claim? After the mint, when the claim opens. The date is announced on X and here.

What if I sell my block? An unclaimed block carries its claim to the buyer. A claimed block has no claim left.

What happens to unclaimed $CITY? It is burned when the claim window closes.
